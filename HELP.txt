SWITCH GAMES — PLAYER GUIDE
Updated 24 October 2026

GETTING STARTED
Choose a game, read its instructions and select Start Game. The instructions
show the controls for the selected one- or two-switch mode. Use Switches on
that screen to change this game only, and Slower/Faster to set its speed (1–10).
The global Switches setting changes the default for all games and clears
per-game switch-mode overrides. Per-game speed settings are retained.

INPUT AND MENUS
Switch 1: Left Arrow, number 1 or number 3.
Switch 2: Right Arrow, number 2 or number 4.
Touch: use the two large buttons below the playing area; hold or tap as the
game instructions describe. Tapping elsewhere is not a gameplay switch.
Keyboard: Tab/Shift+Tab moves focus; Enter/Space activates a focused button.
Space/Enter away from a button acts as switch 1. On a game control button,
Space/Enter operates that button. Arrow/number keys remain switch inputs.
Gamepads: buttons 2/3, D-pad left or left-stick left operate switch 1;
buttons 0/1, D-pad right or left-stick right operate switch 2. Numbering is
the browser's Gamepad API numbering; physical labels vary by controller.

Automatic scanning advances the menu highlight on a timer; either switch
chooses it. Step scanning uses switch 2 for Next and switch 1 to Choose.
Scanning Off leaves ordinary keyboard, mouse and touch navigation available.
Pressing Tab suspends scanning until the next switch input.
The first launch uses one-switch mode and automatic scanning. Choosing the
global two-switch profile selects step scanning. You can change scanning
independently. With scanning off and no game running, hold either switch for
2.5 seconds to restore automatic scanning and open Settings.

PAUSE AND RETURN
Use Pause, P, or a long switch hold to pause. P toggles pause/resume; Resume
continues the game. Hold-to-pause defaults to 2.5 seconds; Settings cycles
through Off, 1.5, 2.5, 4 and 6 seconds. Turn it off or lengthen it for games
that need continuous thrust, movement or flipper holds.
Escape/Home closes game instructions, returns from Settings or Language,
or returns to the game list, depending on the current screen. During play
it returns to the game list. The Main Menu button does the same.
Leaving the browser tab or window automatically pauses and clears held input.

SETTINGS
Scanning speed sets the menu interval (1–10 seconds in the button's choices)
and Simon's one-switch answer interval. It does not set gameplay speed.
Repeat filter ignores rapid repeat presses on the same logical switch; it
does not require a press to be held for a minimum duration.
Practice recovers from losses while retaining score and level. Some rounds
or hazards reset; practice scores can still become high scores.
Sound controls game effects. Spoken names is separate and controls optional
device-voice announcements of scanned choices and changing game cues.
For silence, turn BOTH Sound and Spoken names off.
Large text enlarges interface text. Less motion reduces interface animation
and record confetti; it does not stop moving gameplay or make it flash-free.
Language changes the interface and instructions. English game instructions
have been reviewed for this update; other translations still need review by
native speakers against the current game controls.

SAVING AND OFFLINE USE
Settings, language and best scores are saved in this browser when local
storage is available. If storage is blocked, you can still play, but changes
may not survive closing the page. Clearing site data removes saved choices.
On HTTPS or localhost the app caches its files after a successful online
load. Wait for the initial cache to finish before relying on offline use.
Updates wait for old game tabs to close before activating; reconnect and
close/reopen old tabs to load an update. Opening index.html as a local file
does not provide the same service-worker/offline installation behavior.

GAME CONTROLS
In one-switch mode either logical switch can perform the single action.
The per-game instructions below describe current English behavior.

SPACE-INVADERS
Destroy the aliens. Your ship fires automatically.
One switch: Your ship moves automatically. Press either switch to reverse direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to move left; hold switch 2 to move right.

SNAKE
Eat food to grow. Avoid walls and your own body.
One switch: Press either switch to turn right relative to the direction the snake is travelling.
Two switches: Press switch 1 to turn left; press switch 2 to turn right, relative to the snake.

BREAKOUT
Bounce the ball off your paddle to break the bricks.
One switch: The paddle moves automatically. Press either switch to reverse direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to move left; hold switch 2 to move right.

BUBBLE-SHOOTER
Match at least three bubbles of the same colour. Clear the board before bubbles reach the launcher.
One switch: The launcher aims automatically. Press either switch to fire when it points where you want.
Two switches: The launcher aims automatically. Press either switch to fire when it points where you want.

TETRIS
Fill complete horizontal rows. Pieces fall automatically.
One switch: The piece moves left and right automatically. Press either switch to rotate it.
Two switches: Press switch 1 to move left; pressing at the left edge wraps to the right if there is room. Press switch 2 to rotate.

PONG
Return the ball past the computer paddle. Each goal earns 10 score points; conceding five goals ends the game.
One switch: Your paddle moves up and down automatically. Press either switch to reverse direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to move up; hold switch 2 to move down.

FLAPPY
Fly through the gaps between pipes without crashing.
One switch: Press either switch to flap. Release and press again for another flap.
Two switches: Press either switch to flap. Release and press again for another flap.

DINO
Avoid obstacles as the dinosaur runs automatically.
One switch: Press either switch to jump. This mode has ground obstacles and no birds.
Two switches: Press switch 1 to jump; hold switch 2 to duck under birds.

HELICOPTER
Fly through the cave without hitting its walls.
One switch: Hold either switch to thrust upward. Release to fall.
Two switches: Hold either switch to thrust upward. Release both to fall.

RHYTHM
Press as notes reach the hit line. Avoid missing notes and build combos.
One switch: Notes use one lane. Press either switch in time with each note.
Two switches: Press switch 1 for a left-lane note; press switch 2 for a right-lane note.

TANK
Destroy enemies. Your tank fires automatically.
One switch: Your tank turns automatically. Press either switch to reverse its turning direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to turn left; hold switch 2 to turn right.

ASTEROIDS
Destroy asteroids. Large asteroids split into smaller ones. Your ship fires automatically.
One switch: Your ship turns automatically. Press either switch to reverse its turning direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to turn left; hold switch 2 to turn right.

SKIING
Steer through gates and avoid trees as you ski downhill.
One switch: The skier steers automatically. Press either switch to reverse direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to steer left; hold switch 2 to steer right.

BALANCE
Keep the ball on the platform.
One switch: The platform tilts automatically. Press either switch to reverse the tilt direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to tilt left; hold switch 2 to tilt right.

SIMON
Watch the left/right sequence, then repeat it in order.
One switch: Wait for Your turn. The left/right answer highlight alternates automatically; press either switch to choose it. Scanning speed sets the answer interval.
Two switches: Wait for Your turn. Press switch 1 for left or switch 2 for right, matching the sequence.

WHACK
Hit moles while they are up. The round lasts 30 seconds of game time.
One switch: Press either switch while a mole is up to hit an available mole.
Two switches: Press switch 1 for the left hole; press switch 2 for the right hole.

AIRHOCKEY
Hit the puck into the computer goal and defend your own. Conceding five goals ends the game; scoring five goals advances the level.
One switch: Your paddle moves up and down automatically. Press either switch to reverse direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to move up; hold switch 2 to move down.

LANDER
Land gently on the green pad before fuel runs out. There is no left/right steering.
One switch: Hold either switch to fire upward rockets and slow your fall. Release to descend.
Two switches: Hold either switch to fire upward rockets and slow your fall. Release both to descend.

FRUIT
Slice fruit before it falls away. Five missed fruit end the game.
One switch: Press either switch to slash both sides.
Two switches: Press switch 1 to slash left; press switch 2 to slash right.

STACK
Stack the moving blocks. Overhanging portions are cut off; missing the tower ends the game.
One switch: Press either switch to drop the moving block when it lines up with the tower.
Two switches: Press either switch to drop the moving block when it lines up with the tower.

PENALTY
Shoot past the goalkeeper. Wait for the next ball before shooting again.
One switch: Press either switch to shoot. Shots alternate between left and right.
Two switches: Press switch 1 to shoot left; press switch 2 to shoot right.

PINBALL
Keep the ball in play and hit bumpers to score. You start with three balls.
One switch: Hold either switch to lift both flippers. Release to lower them.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 for the left flipper; hold switch 2 for the right flipper.

BRICKBUILDER
Place blocks to fill complete rows and clear them.
One switch: The block moves left and right automatically. Press either switch to drop it into the highlighted position.
Two switches: Press switch 1 to move left; press switch 2 to move right. The block drops automatically after a short interval.

SPACEDODGE
Avoid asteroids and survive as long as possible.
One switch: Your ship moves automatically. Press either switch to reverse direction.
Two switches: Hold switch 1 to move left; hold switch 2 to move right.

WIRELOOP
Guide the ring along the wire. Moving too quickly through bends can cost a life.
One switch: Hold either switch to move forward. Release to stop and let the ring settle.
Two switches: Hold switch 2 to move forward; hold switch 1 to move back more slowly. Release both to stop.

ACCESSIBILITY NOTES
Menus and dialogs support switch scanning and ordinary keyboard navigation.
Game descriptions and periodic score/level updates are exposed to assistive
technology; Simon, Whack and Fruit also expose changing game cues. Fast
canvas games still require game-specific nonvisual and real-switch testing.
Choose a comfortable speed and input setup with the player.

LOCAL DEVELOPMENT
Use npm start, then open http://127.0.0.1:8080. npm start builds automatically.
Run npm test for regression checks. See TESTING.txt for browser-test commands
and CHROME_PLAYTHROUGH.txt for the historical October 2026 gameplay audit.
